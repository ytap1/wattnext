## Value soundbite (defensible — state the assumptions if a shark probes)

> "Per event: two emergency truck-rolls consolidated into one — about **$2,400** — plus **90
> exposure-minutes** cut and a vulnerable household reached before disaster. Now multiply that across
> every main."

If pressed on the numbers, hold this honest ground:
> "$1,200 loaded cost per emergency dispatch, and we **deliberately don't** put a dollar figure on the
> safety value — we show it as exposure-minutes and the flagged household."

That restraint reads as credibility, not a dodge.

## Demo mechanics (so the beat can't misfire live)

- Drive it from **Call A (Rosa)** → **▶ Play call**. The cross-incident panel and the queue panel
  both appear only after the call plays.
- The **📣 Broadcast** button is in the Queue intelligence panel — scroll to it before you say the
  queue line so it's on screen when you click.
- Everything is deterministic: the cluster and queue panels render **even if the live AI call or wifi
  fails** (the app falls back automatically). The beat is safe on venue wifi.
- Optional evidence flex: sidebar **🧪 Debug → "Show prompt sent to Gemini"** proves the cross-incident
  context is actually fed into the one real AI call — good for a technical shark.
